# Intake questionnaire

Send this as a Google Form, a Tally form or a document. The section headings match the fields in `venue.json`, so the agent can transfer the answers directly.

1. Venue name, exactly as you want it written:
2. What kind of place are you, in a few words? (e.g. "Café & bakery", "Riverside pub & kitchen")
3. Address and postcode:
4. Phone number for customers:
5. Email address for customers:
6. Instagram, Facebook and TikTok handles:
7. Who should we contact about the website (name, mobile, email)? And who makes the final decisions?

## 2. Opening hours
8. Normal opening hours for each day, including any days you're closed:
9. Kitchen or food-service hours, if they're different:
10. Any closures or seasonal hours coming up in the next 3 months (Christmas, holidays)?

## 3. Menu and food
11. Please send your current menu or menus (photo, PDF or a link).
12. Which items are vegetarian, vegan or gluten-free? **We only publish dietary tags you confirm.**
13. Exactly how should we word your allergen statement? (e.g. "Please tell us about allergies before ordering. Our kitchen handles all 14 major allergens.")
14. How often does your menu change? (weekly, monthly, seasonally)

## 4. Bookings and enquiries
15. How do people book? (booking system and link, phone, walk-in only)
16. Do you do private hire, events or catering? If so: spaces, capacity, and who should enquiries go to?
17. Do you sell gift vouchers or take online orders? With which provider?

## 5. Your story and style
18. In 2–3 sentences, what makes you different? Tell us like you'd tell a regular.
19. Three words that describe the feel of your place:
20. Any websites you like the look of, from any business?
21. Colours, fonts or logo files we should use:
22. Anything you definitely **don't** want?

## 6. Regular events
23. Any regular events (quiz night, live music, supper clubs)? What, when, how much?

## 7. Common questions
24. What do customers ask you most often? (dogs, parking, accessibility, kids, gluten-free…)

## 8. Access (we'll help with any of this at the kickoff)
25. Do you already have a domain or website? Who registered it? Do you have the login?
26. Who has access to your Google Business Profile?
27. Are you happy for us to use photos from your Instagram or Google profile?
