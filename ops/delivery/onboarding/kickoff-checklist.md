# Kickoff checklist (20 minutes at the venue)

Bring your phone, charged, for photos.

**Before you go** 🤖
- [ ] Read the intake answers and list what's missing.
- [ ] Look the venue up on Google and Instagram. Note any differences in hours or information.

**At the venue** 👤
- [ ] Go through what's missing from the intake. Confirm the dietary tags and the allergen wording.
- [ ] **Domain:** find out who has it. If the owner can log in, add the DNS records together now, or note the registrar.
- [ ] **Google Business Profile:** if they're on Plus or Group, ask them to add you as a Manager (not Owner). If they're on Core, note any wrong information so you can fix it together at launch.
- [ ] Take 10–20 photos in natural light: the outside, the room, the counter or bar, 3–5 signature dishes or drinks, and the team if they're willing.
- [ ] Ask them how they'll send edit requests (WhatsApp or email), and save their number.
- [ ] Explain what counts as a change and what the turnaround is for their plan, in about a minute.
- [ ] Agree the review day (Day 3) and the target launch day (Day 5).
- [ ] Ask: "Who else needs to see the site before it goes live?" Get them on the review message.

**After** 🤖
- [ ] Upload the photos to the client repo (`public/images/`) and fill in `venue.json`.
- [ ] Log anything still missing as a GitHub issue labelled `waiting-on-client`.
- [ ] Send a recap: "Great to see you. Here's what I still need: …"
