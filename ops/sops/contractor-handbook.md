# Contractor handbook

Welcome! Running Tab builds and looks after websites for independent cafés, pubs and restaurants in Cambridge, for a monthly fee. This handbook tells you how we work. The detailed step-by-step checklists are linked below.

## Your role (site producer)
- Onboarding visits: the kickoff checklist, taking photos, and collecting logins.
- Building and launching client sites from the template, using the 5-day launch SOP.
- Handling edit requests within the turnaround times.
- Monthly maintenance checks and drafting reports.
- **You don't** set prices, agree discounts, approve allergen or legal text, or handle complaints. Pass those to the founder.

## Our standards
1. **Accuracy first.** Copy prices, allergen statements and dietary tags *exactly* as the client gave them. If anything is unclear, ask the client; don't guess.
2. **On time.** Turnaround promises are in `ops/delivery/edits/edit-workflow.md`. If you're going to miss one, tell the founder before it happens.
3. **Only what was asked.** Out-of-scope work gets quoted, never done quietly for free.
4. **Kind and plain.** Short, friendly messages in UK English. Venue owners are busy.
5. **Leave a trail.** Every request gets a GitHub issue and a row in `edit-log.csv`, and you log your hours in `ops/time-log.csv`.

## What needs the founder's sign-off
| You can do it yourself | Needs the founder |
|---|---|
| Hours, closures, banners and event changes (once the client has been live 2 weeks) | Any menu or price change, until you've been signed off on it |
| Photo swaps, small text fixes, broken links | Allergen statements, terms, anything legal-sounding |
| Monthly checks and minor dependency updates | Major upgrades, template changes |
| Drafting reports and messages | Sending a message to a *new* contact or prospect, quotes, discounts, complaints |

## Access (least privilege)
- **You get:** GitHub access to the client repos you're working on, Cloudflare Pages access for previews and deploys, UptimeRobot (read-only), and the shared delivery inbox or WhatsApp.
- **You don't get:** Stripe, domain registrar accounts, or the founder's email.
- Use a password manager and turn on two-factor authentication everywhere. Never share logins by message.
- When the engagement ends, all your access is removed the same day.

## Data protection
- We're a data processor for our clients' enquiry forms, and you'll see some personal data (names, emails, enquiries). Only use it for the task in front of you. Never copy it to personal devices or accounts, and never share it.
- You'll sign a short **contractor agreement**, with confidentiality and data processing terms, before you get any access. ⚖️ The founder should have a solicitor check it.
- Report anything that looks like a data breach (a lost phone, a wrong email recipient) to the founder **straight away**.

## Hours and pay
- Log your hours daily in `ops/time-log.csv` with the category (delivery, admin or sales) and the client.
- Invoice monthly. You're self-employed and responsible for your own tax. ⚖️ The founder should check IR35 and employment status before taking anyone on.
