# Walk-in routes: week of 16 November

All the venues below are in `ops/crm.csv` (47 leads). The names come from public food guides that are up to about 18 months old, so **some may have closed or moved**. Tick off what you find and tell me, and I'll update the CRM.

**Before you go in:** a quick look at their website (or their Google listing) on your phone helps a lot. I couldn't check the websites from here, so most say `site_status=unknown`, and nobody scores A yet. Your own observations will fix that.

## Pub ownership check (10 seconds each)
Ask: *"Is this place independent, or part of a group?"* If a pub company or brewery runs the website, the manager usually can't buy from us. Note the answer in the CRM (`independent`) and move on.
